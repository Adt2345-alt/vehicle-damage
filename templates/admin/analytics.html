AI Severity Classification Methodology

Each detection produced by YOLOv8 is evaluated in real time using geometric bounding box metrics, detection confidence, and spatial overlap counts:

Minor Severity 0.5× Multiplier
Surface level scratches, small scuffs (<4% bbox area ratio), isolated parts without compound damage.
Moderate Severity 1.0× Multiplier
Medium-size dents, panel deformation (4–12% bbox area ratio), or single overlapping collision point.
Severe Severity 1.8× Multiplier
Deep structural deformation (>12% area), clustered compound fractures, or multiple overlapping impacts.
